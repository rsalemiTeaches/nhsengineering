In this project, you will use a digital pin to read whether a switch is being pushed. You will use the result to create a light show.

Your light show can look like the example here, or you can be more creative.

# The circuit

The circuit has two pieces: the switch and the LEDs

## The Switch

Wire a resistor so that it connects to GND. Connect the other end to 5V through a switch. Now, when you press the switch, the voltage on the resistor goes from 0 V to 5 V.

Connect the powered side of the switch to a digital pin on the Arduino and use that pin to read the state of the switch. Write code that reads the state of the switch using digitalRead().

Notice that the number in digitalRead matches the pin number.

## The LEDs

The LEDs make up the light show. Connect the LEDs to digital pins so you can turn them on using digitalWrite().

The basepin is either 8 or 11. The lightscount up from the basepin.

![[e04_05.png]]

## Running the circuit

When you run this circuit, the switch controls whether the red or blue lights light up, one after the other.

# Now it is your turn

Your assignment is to use a switch to control a light show. Use **at least six** LEDs.

**Your light show must be a different design than the one above.**
