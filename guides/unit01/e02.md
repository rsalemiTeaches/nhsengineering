In this project, you will use the Arduino as a power source and control an LED using only a switch. You will build this circuit:

![[e02_01.png|354]]

You will use a switch on the breadboard to implement this circuit.

## Switch on a breadboard.

Our switches have two legs that plug into the breadboard. The switch connects the two legs when you press the button.

Here is an example:

![[e02_03.png|345]]

In the example above, one of the legs on the switch is connected to row 10. The other is connected to row 12. When you press the button, row 10 connects to row 12.

Here is a power switch on a breadboard in TinkerCad. It works the same way, but it has two legs on each side.
