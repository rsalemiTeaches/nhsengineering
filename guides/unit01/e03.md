In this project, we will make an LED on a breadboard blink along with the LED on the Arduino board.

# Arduino Pin 13

On an Arduino UNO, the LED_BUILTIN pin number (which you see used in the code) is pin 13. We will use pin 13 to make an LED blink on the breadboard.
